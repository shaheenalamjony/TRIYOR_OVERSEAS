Explore Top Study Destinations

Find your dream study abroad destination. Compare world-class universities, post-study work rights, living costs, and high-value scholarship opportunities.

United States
Top Choice

Study in USA

Home to the world's most prestigious Ivy League institutions, flexible curriculums, and STEM OPT 3-year work extension.

Average Tuition: $18,000 - $45,000 / yr
Work Rights: 20 hrs/wk + up to 3 yrs OPT
Scholarships: Up to 100% Available
United Kingdom
Fast Track

Study in UK

1-Year Master's degrees, Russell Group universities, and 2-Year Graduate Immigration Route (PSW) post-study work visa.

Average Tuition: £12,000 - £26,000 / yr
Work Rights: 20 hrs/wk + 2 yrs PSW
Scholarships: £2,000 - £10,000+
Canada
High Demand

Study in Canada

Globally recognized degrees, welcoming multicultural society, and clear pathway to Permanent Residency (PR) via PGWP.

Average Tuition: CAD 16,000 - 32,000 / yr
Work Rights: 20 hrs/wk + up to 3 yrs PGWP
Scholarships: Merit & Entrance Aid
Australia
Group of Eight

Study in Australia

World-class living standards, Group of Eight top 100 universities, generous work rights, and high graduate salaries.

Average Tuition: AUD 22,000 - 42,000 / yr
Work Rights: 48 hrs/fortnight + 2-4 yrs PSW
Scholarships: 20% to 50% Tuition Fee
Finland & Denmark
Nordic Quality

Study in Finland

Happiest country in the world, high-tech innovation, safe society, and generous post-graduation work opportunities.

Average Tuition: €8,000 - €14,000 / yr
Work Rights: 30 hrs/wk + 2 yrs job seeker visa
Scholarships: Up to 100% Tuition Waiver
Germany
Low Tuition

Study in Germany

Europe’s industrial powerhouse offering tuition-free or ultra-low tuition universities with outstanding engineering and business programs.

Average Tuition: €0 - €3,000 / yr (Public)
Work Rights: 140 full / 280 half days + 18 mo PSW
Scholarships: DAAD & Merit Grants
Malaysia & Korea
Affordable

Study in Malaysia

Affordable living, UK/Australian branch campuses, English-taught programs, and hassle-free visa processing with fast turnaround.

Average Tuition: $4,000 - $9,000 / yr
Living Cost: Very Low ($300-$500/mo)
Degree Type: UK / AU Dual Degrees
Ireland
Tech Hub

Study in Ireland

European headquarters of Google, Apple, and Pfizer. English-speaking nation with exceptional IT and pharma post-study careers.

Average Tuition: €10,000 - €22,000 / yr
Work Rights: 20 hrs/wk + 2 yrs Graduate Stamp 1G
Scholarships: Government & Uni Aid
New Zealand
Safe & Scenic

Study in New Zealand

All 8 universities in top 3% globally. Safe, welcoming environment with 3-year post-study work visa eligibility for masters grads.

Average Tuition: NZD 24,000 - 38,000 / yr
Work Rights: 20 hrs/wk + up to 3 yrs PSW
Scholarships: Manaaki & Uni Awards
